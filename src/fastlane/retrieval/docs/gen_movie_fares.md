# Movie Ticket Prices and Show Times

Every screen runs four shows a day at 10 AM, 1:30 PM, 5 PM, 8:30 PM. Ticket prices per seat, including GST:

| Category | Weekday | Weekday morning (10 AM) | Fri-Sun and festivals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | Rs 180 | Rs 150 | Rs 220 |
| Prime | Rs 250 | Rs 220 | Rs 290 |
| Recliner | Rs 460 | Rs 460 | Rs 500 |

Morning-show discounts apply to Classic and Prime only, on every day (so a Saturday 10 AM Classic ticket is Rs 190). Cancellation fee: Rs 30 per ticket.

## Screens

- **Screen 1**: 140 seats (10 rows of 14). classic rows A-E; prime rows F-I; recliner rows J.
- **Screen 2**: 96 seats (8 rows of 12). classic rows A-D; prime rows E-G; recliner rows H.
- **Screen 3**: 60 seats (6 rows of 10). classic rows A-C; prime rows D-E; recliner rows F.
