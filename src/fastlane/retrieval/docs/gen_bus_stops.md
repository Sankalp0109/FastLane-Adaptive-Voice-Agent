# Bus Stops and Pick-up Points

Buses pick up and drop at the points below, in order. Times are approximate offsets from departure. Return buses stop at the same points in reverse order. Tell the assistant your pick-up point when booking.

## Hyderabad-Bengaluru

1. Ameerpet Metro (start)
2. Kukatpally Y Junction (+20 min)
3. Shamshabad toll plaza (+55 min)
4. Jadcherla (+1 h 50 min, meal stop at Highway Dhaba)
5. Kurnool bypass (+3 h 45 min)
6. Anantapur (+6 h 10 min)
7. Hebbal, Bengaluru (+9 h 15 min)
8. Majestic Bus Stand (end)

## Hyderabad-Vijayawada

1. LB Nagar Ring Road (start)
2. Hayathnagar (+15 min)
3. Choutuppal (+45 min)
4. Suryapet (+2 h 10 min, meal stop)
5. Nandigama (+4 h 10 min)
6. Pandit Nehru Bus Station (end)

## Hyderabad-Chennai

1. Ameerpet Metro (start)
2. Mehdipatnam (+25 min)
3. Shamshabad (+55 min)
4. Kurnool (+3 h 50 min, meal stop)
5. Kadapa (+6 h 30 min)
6. Tirupati bypass (+8 h 15 min)
7. Koyambedu, Chennai (end)

## Boarding point facilities

Ameerpet Metro: covered waiting area under the metro station, washrooms in the station. LB Nagar: open lay-by near the ring road, a tea stall. Majestic, Koyambedu and Pandit Nehru Bus Station: platform numbers are sent by SMS 2 hours before departure.
