# Cinema Snacks, Combos and Allergens

Snack counters at all four cinemas sell the items below. Moonbeam Cinemas Screen 1 (recliners) offers seat service: press the call button. Allergens are listed per item; popcorn is popped in coconut oil.

## Snacks

- **Salted Popcorn (regular)** - Rs 180. Vegan. Allergens: none listed.
- **Salted Popcorn (large)** - Rs 260. Vegan. Allergens: none listed.
- **Caramel Popcorn (large)** - Rs 300. Vegetarian. Allergens: dairy.
- **Cheese Popcorn (large)** - Rs 320. Vegetarian. Allergens: dairy.
- **Peri-Peri Fries** - Rs 190. Vegan. Allergens: none listed.
- **Veg Puff** - Rs 90. Vegetarian. Allergens: gluten, dairy.
- **Chicken Puff** - Rs 110. Non-vegetarian. Allergens: gluten, egg, dairy.
- **Paneer Tikka Sandwich** - Rs 220. Vegetarian. Allergens: gluten, dairy, mustard.
- **Chicken Club Sandwich** - Rs 260. Non-vegetarian. Allergens: gluten, egg, dairy.
- **Nachos with Cheese** - Rs 240. Vegetarian. Allergens: dairy.
- **Samosa (2 pcs)** - Rs 120. Vegan. Allergens: gluten.
- **Chocolate Brownie** - Rs 160. Vegetarian. Allergens: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts.
- **Cold Coffee** - Rs 180. Vegetarian. Allergens: dairy.
- **Soft Drink (regular)** - Rs 120. Vegan. Allergens: none listed.
- **Mineral Water** - Rs 40. Vegan. Allergens: none listed.

## Combos

- **Couple Combo** - 2 regular salted popcorn + 2 soft drinks, Rs 420.
- **Family Combo** - 2 large popcorn (any flavour) + 4 soft drinks + nachos, Rs 990.
- **Kids Combo** - regular salted popcorn + juice box + chocolate, Rs 260.

## Pre-ordering snacks

Snacks cannot be added to a phone booking. They are bought at the counter or ordered from the seat in recliner screens.
