# Movies - Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I choose my exact seats?

Not by phone. The system picks the best block of adjacent seats in the category you choose. If you
want particular seats, book at the box office or online. If you have a preference such as an aisle
seat, tell the assistant; it is passed to the cinema as a note but cannot be guaranteed.

## Can I bring my child to an A-certified film?

No. A-certified films are for viewers aged 18 and above, and this includes infants. Cinema staff can
refuse entry and the ticket is not refunded.

## Do children need a ticket?

Children aged 3 and above need a full-price ticket. Below 3 they may sit on a parent's lap, except in
A-certified films.

## Can I take my ticket to another cinema?

No. A ticket is for one show at one cinema. To move to another cinema, change the booking (allowed up
to 4 hours before the show).

## What happens if I am late?

The show starts on time after about 10 minutes of advertisements. Late entry is allowed until the
interval, but there is no refund for missed time.

## Is there an interval?

Yes, every film longer than 100 minutes has a 15-minute interval.

## Can I get a refund if I did not like the film?

No. Refunds follow the cancellation policy only.

## Do you show films in 3D or IMAX?

No. All our screens are 2D with Dolby 7.1 sound.

## Can I book a whole screen for a private show?

Private screenings, birthday shows and corporate bookings are arranged by staff. The assistant will
transfer you.

## The film I want is not listed.

We show only the films listed in "Films Now Showing". Older films leave screens on Thursdays; new
releases open on Fridays.

## Are subtitles available?

All non-English films have English subtitles.

## Is there a dress code?

No dress code. Footwear is required.

## Can I carry a power bank or a laptop?

Yes, laptops and power banks are allowed but must stay switched off during the show. Cameras and
tripods are not allowed.

## Is there a discount for students or senior citizens?

Only at the box office with valid ID; phone bookings are at standard prices.

## Do you sell gift vouchers?

Gift vouchers are sold at the box office only and cannot be redeemed for phone bookings.
