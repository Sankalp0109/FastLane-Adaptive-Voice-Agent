# FastLane Phone Booking - Frequently Asked Questions

## Am I talking to a person?

No. FastLane is an automated booking assistant. It can book, change and cancel movie tickets, bus
tickets and restaurant tables, and answer questions about our policies. Callers can ask for a staff
member at any time.

## What can the assistant not do?

It cannot take card or UPI payments over the phone, process refund disputes, handle complaints,
or book anything outside our cinemas, bus routes and restaurants (for example flights, trains,
hotels or other restaurants). These requests are transferred to staff.

## How do I pay?

Movie and bus tickets booked by phone are paid at the box office or at boarding, or through the
payment link in the confirmation SMS. Restaurant deposits are paid through an SMS payment link.
Prices include GST. There is no convenience fee on phone bookings.

## Will I get a confirmation?

Yes. Every booking, change and cancellation is confirmed by SMS to the mobile number on the booking,
with the booking reference.

## I lost my booking reference.

If you call from the mobile number used for the booking, the assistant can find your upcoming
bookings without the reference. From another number, staff can help after checking your identity.

## How are dates like "next Friday" understood?

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. "This Friday" or just "Friday" means the coming Friday. "Next Friday"
means the Friday of next week. Because people use these words differently, the assistant always
reads back the full date (for example "Friday, 23 October") before booking, so it can be corrected.
"This weekend" means the coming Saturday and Sunday.

## How far ahead can I book?

Shows, buses and tables can be booked up to 30 days ahead.

## Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Give their name for the booking and, for buses, each passenger's name, age and gender. Changes
and cancellations later need the mobile number used for the booking.

## Do you have loyalty points or discounts?

We do not run a loyalty programme. Student and senior-citizen discounts are available only at the
cinema box office with valid ID, not on phone bookings.

## Which languages does the assistant speak?

The assistant speaks English and understands common Hindi and Telugu words. For a full conversation
in Hindi or Telugu, ask for a staff member.

## How do I make a complaint?

Complaints are handled by staff. The assistant transfers the call with a short summary so you do not
have to repeat yourself.
