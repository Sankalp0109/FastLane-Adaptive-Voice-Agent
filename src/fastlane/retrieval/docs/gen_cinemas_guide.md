# Cinema Locations, Parking and Facilities

## Galaxy Cinemas Kukatpally

Address: 4th floor, Nexa Mall, KPHB Road, Kukatpally, Hyderabad. Three screens. Mall parking (Rs 60 for 4 hours). Food court on the same floor. Nearest metro: KPHB Colony.

## Starlight Multiplex Gachibowli

Address: Sarath Arcade, Old Mumbai Highway, Gachibowli, Hyderabad. Three screens. Free basement parking for 3 hours with a ticket. Cafe in the lobby. Nearest metro: none; cabs recommended.

## Regal Talkies Ameerpet

Address: Opposite Metro Pillar 1102, Ameerpet, Hyderabad. Three screens. No dedicated parking; use the metro parking lot opposite (Rs 40). Single-level, older building with lifts. Nearest metro: Ameerpet.

## Moonbeam Cinemas Secunderabad

Address: SD Road, near Paradise Circle, Secunderabad, Hyderabad. Three screens. Paid parking (Rs 50). Recliner lounge with food service in Screen 1. Nearest metro: Paradise.
