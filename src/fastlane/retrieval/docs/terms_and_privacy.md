# Terms of Booking, Call Recording and Privacy

## Who you are booking with

FastLane is the phone booking service for the cinemas, bus routes and restaurants listed in our guides.
The venue or operator is responsible for the service itself.

## The automated assistant

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. It tells you at the start of the call that it is
automated. You can ask for a person at any time during staff hours (9:00-22:00).

## Call recording

Calls are recorded for quality and to resolve disputes. Recordings are kept for 30 days and then
deleted. Transcripts without your phone number may be kept for up to 12 months to improve the service.

## What we store

We store your name, mobile number and booking details, and for bus passengers the names, ages and
genders given at booking. We do not store card or UPI details; payments happen through the payment
partner's link.

## Using your booking history

When you call from a number we know, the assistant can see your past and upcoming bookings to save you
time (for example, "the same as last time"). It never reads out another person's bookings.

## Your rights

You can ask staff for a copy of the data we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, except where we
must keep booking records for tax purposes (8 years).

## SMS messages

We send SMS only for bookings, changes, cancellations, payment links and service alerts. We do not send
marketing messages.

## Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. The version that applies is the one in effect on the day of booking.
