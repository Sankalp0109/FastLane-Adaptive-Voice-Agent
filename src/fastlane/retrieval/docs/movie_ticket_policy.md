# Movie Ticket Booking, Change and Cancellation Policy

This policy covers tickets booked by phone for Galaxy Cinemas Kukatpally, Starlight Multiplex
Gachibowli, Regal Talkies Ameerpet and Moonbeam Cinemas Secunderabad.

## How many tickets can be booked

A single booking can hold at most 10 tickets. All tickets in one booking are for the same show
and the same seat category. Groups of more than 10 people (school trips, office outings) are
handled by our group desk; the phone agent transfers such callers to staff, who can hold a block
of seats and arrange an invoice.

Every person entering the auditorium needs a ticket, including children aged 3 and above.
Children below 3 years may sit on a parent's lap without a ticket, except for films certified A.

## Seat categories

Each screen has three categories:

- **Classic** - front rows. Lowest price.
- **Prime** - middle and rear rows with the best view of the screen.
- **Recliner** - the last row, with powered recliners, a side table and call-button service.

Seats are assigned automatically as a block of adjacent seats in the chosen category, as close to
the centre of the row as possible. If no block of adjacent seats is left, the seats are the closest
free seats in that category and the caller is told they are split. Specific seat numbers cannot be
promised by phone.

## When booking closes

Phone and online sales for a show stop 15 minutes before the show starts. After that, tickets can
only be bought at the box office, subject to availability.

## Changing a booking

A booking can be changed to another show, date, cinema or seat category, or the number of tickets
can be changed, up to **4 hours before the original show time**. The change is subject to seat
availability. If the new tickets cost more, the difference is collected at the box office; if they
cost less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days.

Changes within 4 hours of the show are not possible. The caller may instead buy new tickets and the
original tickets are not refunded.

## Cancelling a booking

Tickets can be cancelled up to **4 hours before the show time**. A cancellation fee of
**Rs 30 per ticket** is deducted and the remaining amount is refunded to the original payment
method within 5-7 working days.

Cancellations within 4 hours of the show, and no-shows, are not refunded. Partial cancellation
(cancelling some tickets of a booking) is treated as a change of ticket count and follows the same
4-hour rule.

If a show is cancelled by the cinema (technical failure, power cut), the full ticket price is
refunded automatically, including the convenience fee, and the caller receives an SMS.

## Identity check for changes and cancellations

To change or cancel, the caller must give the booking reference (for example MV7K2Q9) and the
mobile number used for the booking. If they call from that mobile number, the reference alone is
enough to find their bookings. We never reveal or change a booking for someone who cannot give the
matching mobile number; such callers are transferred to staff.

## Film certificates

- **U** - suitable for all ages.
- **UA** - children below 12 should watch with a parent or guardian.
- **A** - adults only (18+). Cinema staff may ask for photo ID at entry; tickets are not refunded if
  entry is refused because of the certificate.

## Food, bags and conduct

Outside food and drinks are not allowed, except baby food and water for infants. Large bags are
checked at security. Smoking, vaping and recording the film are prohibited.

## Accessibility

All four cinemas have step-free access to every screen and wheelchair spaces in the Classic rows of
Screen 1 and Screen 2. Callers who need a wheelchair space should mention it while booking; the
agent notes it on the booking and staff confirm by SMS.
