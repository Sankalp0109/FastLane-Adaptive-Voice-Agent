# Payments and Refund Processing

## How bookings are paid

- **Movie tickets**: pay through the SMS payment link within 30 minutes, or at the box office at least
  30 minutes before the show. Unpaid tickets are released 30 minutes before the show.
- **Bus tickets**: pay through the SMS payment link within 2 hours, or in cash at boarding for trips
  departing within 24 hours.
- **Restaurant deposits**: pay through the SMS payment link within 2 hours, or the reservation is
  released.

The automated assistant cannot take card, UPI or net-banking details on the call and will never ask
for an OTP or PIN. Anyone asking for an OTP in our name is not from FastLane.

## How long refunds take

Refunds go back to the original payment method: UPI in 2-3 working days, cards and net banking in 5-7
working days. Refunds for cash payments are collected at the venue counter with the booking SMS.

## Refund amounts

Refund amounts follow the cancellation policy of each service: the movie ticket policy, the bus
cancellation and refund policy that applied when the ticket was booked, and the restaurant reservation
policy for deposits.

## Refund not received or wrong amount

Payment disputes, missing refunds and double charges are handled by the payments team through staff.
The assistant transfers such calls with the booking reference.
