# Tandoor Tales Table Reservation Policy

Applies to the three Tandoor Tales branches: Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills and Hitech City.

## Seatings and opening hours

Tables are reserved for a **90-minute seating**. Seatings start every half hour:

- **Lunch:** first seating 12:00, last seating 14:30.
- **Dinner:** first seating 19:00, last seating 22:00 at Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills, and 21:30 at
  Hitech City.

Tandoor Tales Banjara Hills is **closed on Mondays**. The other branches are open every day,
including festivals.

Reservations must be made at least 30 minutes before the seating time.

## Party size limits

Phone reservations are accepted for:

- Jubilee Hills: up to 12 guests (the 12-seat private dining room is used for parties of 9-12).
- Banjara Hills: up to 10 guests (10-seat private room).
- Hitech City: up to 8 guests.

Larger parties, birthday events and corporate dinners are handled by our events team; the phone
agent transfers such callers to staff.

## Deposit for large groups

A deposit is needed to hold a table for large groups:

- Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills: parties of **8 or more**, **Rs 300 per guest**.
- Hitech City: parties of **6 or more**, **Rs 250 per guest**.

The deposit is paid through a payment link sent by SMS and must be paid within 2 hours, otherwise
the reservation is released. The deposit is adjusted against the final bill. **Deposits cannot be
waived by the phone agent**; only the branch manager can waive one, so such requests go to staff.

## Changing or cancelling a reservation

Reservations can be changed (time, date, branch or number of guests) or cancelled up to **2 hours**
before the seating, subject to availability. Within 2 hours, please call the branch directly.

A deposit is refunded in full if the reservation is cancelled at least **24 hours** before the
seating. Cancellations within 24 hours forfeit the deposit.

If a change increases a party to the deposit threshold, a deposit becomes due for the new size.

## Late arrival

Tables are held for **15 minutes** after the seating time. After that, the table may be given to
waiting guests.

## Special requests

Callers can ask for indoor or outdoor seating (outdoor at Jubilee Hills and Hitech City only), a high
chair, a cake to be brought out, or dietary needs. Special requests are noted but not guaranteed,
except dietary and allergy notes, which are passed to the kitchen.

Guests may bring their own celebration cake; a cake-cutting fee of Rs 300 applies. Outside food and
drinks are otherwise not allowed. Tandoor Tales does not serve alcohol and does not allow
bring-your-own alcohol.
