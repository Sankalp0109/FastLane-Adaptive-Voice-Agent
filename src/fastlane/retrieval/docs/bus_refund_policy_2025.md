# Bus Ticket Cancellation and Refund Policy - version 2025 (SUPERSEDED)

**This policy was replaced on 1 September 2026.** It still applies only to tickets that were
**booked before 1 September 2026**. For any ticket booked on or after that date, use the current
Bus Ticket Cancellation, Change and Refund Policy.

## Refund when a ticket is cancelled

| Time before departure | Refund of fare |
|---|---|
| More than 48 hours | 85% |
| 12 to 48 hours | 60% |
| 4 to 12 hours | 25% |
| Less than 4 hours | No refund |

## Changing the date of travel

Under this version, date changes were allowed up to 24 hours before departure for a flat change fee
of Rs 100 per seat. (The current policy has no change fee and allows changes up to 12 hours before.)

## Seat limits

At most 6 seats per booking.
