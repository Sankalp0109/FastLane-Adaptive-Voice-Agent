# Bus Ticket Cancellation, Change and Refund Policy

This is the **current** policy. It applies to all bus tickets booked on or after **1 September 2026**.
Tickets booked before that date follow the earlier policy (version 2025) for refunds.

## Refund when a ticket is cancelled

The refund depends on how many hours before the scheduled departure the ticket is cancelled:

| Time before departure | Refund of fare |
|---|---|
| 72 hours or more | 90% |
| 24 to 72 hours | 75% |
| 12 to 24 hours | 50% |
| 4 to 12 hours | 25% |
| Less than 4 hours | No cancellation, no refund |

The percentage is applied to the fare paid for the cancelled seats. Refunds reach the original
payment method within 5-7 working days. Tickets cannot be cancelled within 4 hours of departure;
the seat is then treated as a no-show.

## Changing the date or time of travel

A ticket can be moved to another bus on the **same route** (for example Hyderabad to Bengaluru on a
different date or departure time) up to **12 hours before departure**, subject to seats being
available. If the new fare is higher, the difference is paid at boarding or by payment link; if it
is lower, the difference is refunded. Within 12 hours of departure the ticket cannot be changed and
the cancellation rules above apply instead.

A change of route (for example Bengaluru to Chennai) is a cancellation plus a new booking.

## Passenger details

Every seat must be booked with the passenger's full name, age and gender. Passenger names can be
corrected for spelling up to 12 hours before departure. A ticket cannot be transferred to a
different person.

## Seat limits

At most 6 seats can be booked in one booking. Larger groups should call our charter desk; the phone
agent transfers them to staff.

## Children

Children below 5 years travel free on a parent's lap without a separate seat. Children aged 5 and
above need their own seat at the full fare.

## Missed bus and late arrival

Passengers should reach the boarding point 15 minutes before departure. The bus does not wait for
late passengers, and missed buses are not refunded.

## Bus cancelled by the operator

If we cancel a trip (breakdown, road closure, weather), the full fare is refunded automatically or
the passenger can choose a free move to the next available bus on the same route.
