# Tandoor Tales Allergen and Dietary Guide

This guide lists which dishes are safe for common dietary needs across all branches. Branch menus give the full list with prices. Dishes marked 'Jain option' are made without onion, garlic, potato or other root vegetables on request.

## Nut-free dishes (no tree nuts or peanuts)

Paneer Tikka, Tandoori Aloo, Dahi Ke Kebab, Chilli Baby Corn, Tandoori Chicken (half), Chicken 65, Mutton Seekh Kebab, Apollo Fish, Tandoori Prawns, Egg Bhurji Pav, Dal Makhani, Dal Tadka, Palak Paneer, Kadai Vegetable, Chana Masala, Chicken Chettinad, Mutton Rogan Josh, Goan Fish Curry, Prawn Masala, Tandoori Roti, Butter Naan, Garlic Naan, Lachha Paratha, Jowar Roti, Missi Roti, Jeera Rice, Curd Rice, Gulab Jamun, Fruit Bowl, Masala Chaas, Sweet Lassi, Fresh Lime Soda, Irani Chai, Tender Coconut Water.

## Gluten-free dishes

Paneer Tikka, Tandoori Aloo, Malai Broccoli, Peanut Chaat, Murgh Malai Tikka, Tandoori Chicken (half), Mutton Seekh Kebab, Tandoori Prawns, Dal Makhani, Dal Tadka, Paneer Butter Masala, Palak Paneer, Kadai Vegetable, Chana Masala, Bagara Baingan, Mirchi Ka Salan, Butter Chicken, Chicken Chettinad, Mutton Rogan Josh, Goan Fish Curry, Prawn Masala, Jowar Roti, Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani, Mutton Dum Biryani, Vegetable Dum Biryani, Jeera Rice, Curd Rice, Qubani Ka Meetha, Fruit Bowl, Masala Chaas, Sweet Lassi, Fresh Lime Soda, Irani Chai, Tender Coconut Water.

## Dairy-free dishes

## Egg-free breads

Tandoori Roti, Lachha Paratha, Jowar Roti, Missi Roti.

## Allergen definitions

- **nuts**: tree nuts (cashew, almond, pistachio)
- **peanut**: peanuts
- **gluten**: wheat / gluten
- **dairy**: milk and milk products (ghee, paneer, cream, butter)
- **egg**: egg
- **shellfish**: prawns and crab
- **fish**: fish
- **soy**: soy
- **sesame**: sesame
- **mustard**: mustard

## Notes

The naan dough contains egg; for an egg-free bread choose tandoori roti, jowar roti, lachha paratha or missi roti. Biryanis are garnished with cashew; a nut-free biryani can be made on request with 30 minutes' notice if ordered when booking. The salan served with biryani contains peanuts and sesame.
