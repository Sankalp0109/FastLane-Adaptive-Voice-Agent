# Group Bookings, Private Events and Packages

All group and event bookings below are arranged by staff, not by the automated assistant. The
assistant transfers the caller with a summary of the group size, date and what they want.

## Movie group bookings

For more than 10 tickets, the group desk can hold a block of seats in one category, issue a single
invoice and arrange entry for school groups with a teacher list. Private screenings of a current film
can be booked for an entire screen (Screen 3 seats 60, Screen 2 seats 96, Screen 1 seats 140) on
weekday mornings. Prices are quoted by staff.

## Bus charters

Groups of more than 6 passengers on one trip are handled by the charter desk. Whole-bus charters
(30-berth sleeper or 40-seat coach) are available on our three routes and for one-off trips within
Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

## Restaurant events

- **Private dining rooms**: Jubilee Hills (12 guests) and Banjara Hills (10 guests) can be booked
  through the assistant up to those limits; set menus and decorations are arranged by the events team.
- **Large parties**: parties above the branch limit, up to 40 guests, are seated by closing part of
  the dining room, with a set menu from Rs 950 per guest and a 50% advance.
- **Corporate lunches**: weekday set lunch at Hitech City for 10-40 guests with invoice billing.

## Birthday and anniversary packages

Restaurants can arrange a cake, decorations and a dessert platter for Rs 1,500 with 24 hours' notice.
Cinemas offer a "birthday row" package with seat decorations and popcorn combos for groups of 6-10.

## Payment for groups

Group payments are by invoice, bank transfer or payment link. The assistant cannot take payments.
