# Tandoor Tales - Frequently Asked Questions

## Is the food halal?

Yes. All chicken and mutton served at Tandoor Tales is halal.

## Do you have a separate vegetarian kitchen?

Vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes are cooked in separate sections of the same kitchen with
separate utensils. Jain food is prepared on request without onion, garlic or root vegetables.

## Can you make a dish less spicy?

Most dishes can be made mild on request, except pre-marinated tandoor items and the Chicken 65.

## Do you serve alcohol?

No. Tandoor Tales does not serve alcohol and does not allow guests to bring alcohol.

## Is there a kids' menu?

Yes: mini paneer tikka, butter chicken with jeera rice, and a small gulab jamun, at Rs 220 per
child's plate. High chairs are available at all branches; ask while booking.

## Can I bring a birthday cake?

Yes, with a cake-cutting fee of Rs 300. The branch can also order a cake for you with 24 hours'
notice; ask a staff member.

## Do you deliver or offer takeaway?

Takeaway is available from all branches; call the branch directly. Delivery is through third-party
apps only. The booking assistant handles table reservations only.

## Can I walk in without a booking?

Yes, if a table is free. Weekend dinners are usually fully booked; booking is recommended.

## Is there outdoor seating?

Outdoor terrace tables are available at Jubilee Hills and Hitech City. Banjara Hills is indoor only.
Outdoor seating is closed during heavy rain and the party is moved indoors.

## Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?

Jubilee Hills and Hitech City are step-free. Banjara Hills has two steps at the entrance and a ramp on
request.

## Do you have a buffet?

There is a weekday lunch buffet at Hitech City only (Monday to Friday, Rs 499 per person). Other
branches serve à la carte.

## Do you accept meal vouchers or cards?

All cards, UPI, Sodexo and Pluxee meal cards are accepted at the restaurant. Deposits for large groups
are paid through the SMS payment link.

## Can I order food in advance with my booking?

Only the Raan-e-Sikandari at Jubilee Hills (24 hours' notice) and a nut-free biryani (30 minutes'
notice) can be pre-ordered. Mention it when booking.

## Do you host office parties?

Yes. Groups above the phone-booking limit and set-menu packages are arranged by the events team.
