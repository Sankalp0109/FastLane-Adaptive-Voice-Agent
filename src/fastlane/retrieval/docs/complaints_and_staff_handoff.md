# Complaints, Payments and When Staff Take Over

## Requests always handled by staff

The automated assistant transfers the call to a staff member, with a short summary, when the caller:

- disputes a payment, a deduction or a refund amount, or reports money taken twice;
- wants a refund outside the published rules (for example a cancellation within the cut-off);
- makes a complaint about staff, cleanliness, food quality, a bus driver or a show;
- reports a lost item at a cinema, on a bus or at a restaurant;
- asks for something we do not offer (flights, trains, hotels, other restaurants or cinemas);
- wants a group booking above the limits (more than 10 movie tickets, more than 6 bus seats, or a
  party larger than the branch limit);
- asks for a deposit to be waived;
- cannot give the mobile number of a booking they want to change or cancel;
- asks for a human.

The assistant also hands over when the booking system fails twice in a row.

## What staff can do

Staff can look up any booking after verifying identity, approve exceptions within limits set by the
venue manager, arrange group bookings and invoices, and raise refund tickets with the payments team.

## Response times

Calls are transferred during staff hours, 9:00 to 22:00 every day. Outside these hours the caller is
offered a call-back the next morning and receives an SMS with a ticket number.

## Lost and found

Items found at our cinemas, buses and restaurants are kept for 30 days. Staff can check the lost and
found register and arrange collection.
