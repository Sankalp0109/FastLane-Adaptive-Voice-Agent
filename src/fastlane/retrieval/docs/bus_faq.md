# Buses - Frequently Asked Questions

## How early should I reach the boarding point?

Please reach 15 minutes before departure. Buses leave on time and do not wait for late passengers.
Missed buses are not refunded.

## Can I board at a different point?

Only at the listed boarding points of the route. Intermediate pick-up points are listed in the "Bus
Stops and Pick-up Points" guide. Tell the assistant your pick-up point when booking so it is noted.

## Where will the bus drop me?

At the final stop listed for the route and at the listed drop points on the way, if requested at
boarding.

## Can a woman travelling alone get a seat next to another woman?

Yes. Single women travellers are seated next to women where possible; the assistant notes the
request. On sleeper buses, berths L1-L4 are reserved for women until 6 hours before departure.

## Can I carry extra luggage?

One suitcase up to 15 kg plus a small bag per passenger. Extra luggage is carried if there is space,
at Rs 100 per piece, paid to the conductor.

## Can I bring my pet?

Pets are not allowed, except trained service dogs with papers.

## Is food served on board?

No food is served. Water is provided on AC buses. There is one 20-minute meal stop on long routes.

## Can I change the passenger name?

Spelling corrections are allowed up to 12 hours before departure. A ticket cannot be given to a
different person; cancel and book again instead.

## What if the bus breaks down?

A replacement bus is arranged. If the trip is cancelled you get a full refund or a free move to the
next bus on the same route.

## Do you have buses to Visakhapatnam, Tirupati or Mumbai?

No. We run only Hyderabad-Bengaluru, Hyderabad-Vijayawada and Hyderabad-Chennai, in both directions.

## Can I book a return ticket?

Yes, the return journey is a separate booking on the opposite route.

## Is there a toilet on the bus?

No. Buses stop for a comfort break every 3-4 hours.

## Do I need to print the ticket?

No. Show the SMS with the booking reference and carry a government photo ID.

## Can children travel alone?

Children under 12 must travel with an adult. Passengers aged 12-17 may travel alone with a consent
letter signed by a parent.

## Can I get a GST invoice?

Yes, staff can email a GST invoice for any booking; ask for a staff member.
