# Cinema Seat Maps

All four cinemas use the same three screen layouts. Row A is closest to the screen. Seat 1 is on the left when facing the screen.

## Screen 1 layout

10 rows (A-J), 14 seats per row, 140 seats in all.

- Classic: rows A, B, C, D, E (70 seats)
- Prime: rows F, G, H, I (56 seats)
- Recliner: rows J (14 seats)
- Aisles after seat 7; wheelchair spaces at the end of row A (Screens 1 and 2).

## Screen 2 layout

8 rows (A-H), 12 seats per row, 96 seats in all.

- Classic: rows A, B, C, D (48 seats)
- Prime: rows E, F, G (36 seats)
- Recliner: rows H (12 seats)
- Aisles after seat 6; wheelchair spaces at the end of row A (Screens 1 and 2).

## Screen 3 layout

6 rows (A-F), 10 seats per row, 60 seats in all.

- Classic: rows A, B, C (30 seats)
- Prime: rows D, E (20 seats)
- Recliner: rows F (10 seats)
- Aisles after seat 5; wheelchair spaces at the end of row A (Screens 1 and 2).
